# 🌾 HarvestIQ

### Smarter Harvests. Better Decisions.

HarvestIQ is an AI-powered agricultural decision intelligence platform developed for **HackFusion 2026**. The platform empowers farmers by enabling direct sales to consumers while providing intelligent recommendations to improve profitability and reduce food waste.

---

## 🚀 Problem Statement

Small and marginal farmers often face challenges such as:

- Low selling prices due to intermediaries
- Lack of market price awareness
- Difficulty finding nearby buyers
- Unsold produce leading to food waste
- Consumers paying higher prices

---

## 💡 Our Solution

HarvestIQ connects farmers directly with consumers through a modern web platform while providing smart decision support.

The platform helps farmers:
- 🌱 Sell directly without middlemen
- 💰 Receive fair price recommendations
- 📍 Find nearby buyers
- ♻️ Reduce food waste with freshness alerts

Consumers can:
- 🛒 Browse fresh products
- 📦 Order directly from farmers
- ❤️ Support local agriculture
